# Upper GI Endoscopy with Biopsy - Medical Necessity

Diagnostic upper endoscopy is driven by alarm features or by dyspepsia that has not responded to acid suppression. Age and risk profile matter.

## Indications

- Dysphagia, gastrointestinal bleeding, unintentional weight loss, or iron-deficiency anemia.
- Dyspepsia that persists after 4-8 weeks of proton pump inhibitor therapy.

## Step therapy

For uninvestigated dyspepsia without alarm features, a PPI trial (and H. pylori test-and-treat where relevant) is expected first, particularly under age 60.

## Exclusions

- Uninvestigated dyspepsia under age 60, no alarm features, no PPI trial.
- Surveillance at an interval shorter than guidelines recommend.
