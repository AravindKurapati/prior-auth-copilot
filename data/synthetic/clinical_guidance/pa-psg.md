# Attended Polysomnography - Medical Necessity

Attended in-lab polysomnography is reserved for patients who cannot be evaluated reliably with a home sleep apnea test. Much of this policy needs narrative interpretation, so borderline cases belong with a human reviewer.

## Indications

- Symptoms of obstructive sleep apnea (habitual snoring, witnessed apneas, excessive daytime sleepiness) with at least moderate pretest probability.
- A comorbidity (significant heart failure, COPD, neuromuscular disease) that makes home testing unreliable.

## Step therapy

There is no medication step. The gate is whether a home study is a reasonable first test. If the note does not address that question, the criteria are indeterminate and the request should be interpreted against this guidance rather than auto-decided.

## Exclusions

- Home sleep apnea testing is appropriate and no disqualifying comorbidity exists.
- Repeat study within 12 months without a change in weight or therapy.
