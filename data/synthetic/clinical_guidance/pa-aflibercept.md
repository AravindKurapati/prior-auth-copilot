# Aflibercept Intravitreal Injection - Medical Necessity

Aflibercept is an anti-VEGF agent for specific retinal conditions with confirmed active disease on imaging. Coverage tracks the diagnosis and the imaging evidence, not the drug alone. The patient must have failed or be ineligible for first-line anti-VEGF therapy in many plans; documentation of prior treatments strengthens medical necessity.

## Indications

- Neovascular age-related macular degeneration with subfoveal or juxtafoveal involvement.
- Diabetic macular edema with central retinal thickness >300 micrometers on OCT.
- Macular edema following a branch or central retinal vein occlusion.
- OCT or fluorescein angiography showing active exudation or fluid in the macular region.

## Step therapy

Document prior anti-VEGF agents (bevacizumab, ranibizumab, brolucizumab) attempted in the same eye, the dates and duration of therapy, the response (improvement, stability, progression), and the reason for switching if applicable. A step-through to aflibercept is not universally required but captured history allows the reviewer to assess medical necessity correctly.

## Exclusions

- Active ocular or periocular infection.
- Any indication outside the approved retinal conditions.
