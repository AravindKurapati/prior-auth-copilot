# Aflibercept Intravitreal Injection - Medical Necessity

Aflibercept is an anti-VEGF agent for specific retinal conditions with confirmed active disease on imaging. Coverage tracks the diagnosis and the imaging evidence, not the drug alone.

## Indications

- Neovascular age-related macular degeneration, diabetic macular edema, or macular edema after a retinal vein occlusion.
- OCT or fluorescein angiography showing active exudation or fluid.

## Step therapy

Payers may ask about prior anti-VEGF agents and the response, but a step-through is not universally required; capture what was tried so the reviewer can judge.

## Exclusions

- Active ocular or periocular infection.
- Any indication outside the approved retinal conditions.
