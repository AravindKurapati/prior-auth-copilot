# MRI Lumbar Spine - Medical Necessity

Lumbar MRI is a diagnostic step, not a screening test. It is indicated when a patient has completed a meaningful trial of conservative care and still has symptoms that would change management if a structural cause were found.

## Indications

- Radicular pain or neurologic deficit persisting or worsening after >= 6 weeks of physical therapy, NSAIDs, or activity modification.
- Red-flag features (suspected malignancy, infection, cauda equina, or trauma) at any time, which override the waiting period.

## Step therapy

Document the type of conservative care, the start and end dates, and the response. A vague statement such as "tried PT" without dates is treated as incomplete evidence and should route to the reviewer rather than an automated approval.

## Exclusions

- Acute uncomplicated low back pain of less than 6 weeks with no red flags.
- A repeat study within 12 months when nothing clinically has changed.
