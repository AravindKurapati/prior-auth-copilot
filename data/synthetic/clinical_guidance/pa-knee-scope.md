# Knee Arthroscopy with Meniscectomy - Medical Necessity

Arthroscopic meniscectomy helps patients whose knee mechanically locks or gives way because of a torn meniscus. It does not reliably help degenerative arthritis, so the distinction drives the decision.

## Indications

- Mechanical symptoms (locking, catching, true giving way) that persist despite conservative treatment.
- Imaging or a positive exam consistent with a meniscal tear.

## Step therapy

Expect a documented trial of physical therapy, activity modification, and analgesics over roughly three months before surgery, unless the knee is locked and cannot be straightened.

## Exclusions

- Isolated degenerative osteoarthritis without mechanical symptoms.
- No conservative care attempted in the prior three months.
