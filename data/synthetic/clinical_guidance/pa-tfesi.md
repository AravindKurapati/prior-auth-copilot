# Lumbar Transforaminal Epidural Steroid Injection - Medical Necessity

A lumbar transforaminal epidural steroid injection targets an inflamed nerve root that imaging confirms is compressed and that explains the patient's leg pain.

## Indications

- Dermatomal radicular pain matching an imaging-confirmed level of nerve root compression.
- At least four weeks of conservative therapy without adequate relief.

## Step therapy

Document the conservative measures and pain scores before and after. Track prior injections at the same level; more than three in a rolling twelve months is outside policy.

## Exclusions

- Systemic infection or a bleeding diathesis.
- A fourth same-level injection within twelve months.
