## Case A — `psg_indeterminate` (service 95810, dx G47.33)

1. `criteria_check(service_code="95810", diagnosis_codes=["G47.33"])`
   → `status="indeterminate"` (policy `PA-PSG`) — mechanical check
   cannot verify the narrative conditions.
2. `should_search_guidance("indeterminate")` → **True**
3. `search_clinical_guidance('Is attended in-lab polysomnography medically necessary when a home sleep apnea test is available for suspected obstructive sleep apnea?', service_code="95810")`
   → 4 citation(s):

- **`PA-PSG:lead:0`** — lead · score 0.90
  > Attended in-lab polysomnography is reserved for patients who cannot be evaluated reliably with a home sleep apnea test, or whose home test was technically inadequate or negative despite a strong clinical picture. Home testing is the default first study for uncomplicated suspected obstructive sleep apnea because it is cheaper and more accessible. Much of this policy turns on clinical judgment about pretest probability and comorbidity, so borderline cases with thin documentation belong with a human reviewer rather than an automated decision.
- **`PA-PSG:indications:2`** — Indications · score 0.72
  > A prior negative or technically failed home sleep study with persistent symptoms.
- **`PA-PSG:exclusions:0`** — Exclusions · score 0.71
  > Home sleep apnea testing is appropriate and no disqualifying comorbidity exists.
- **`PA-PSG:step-therapy:0`** — Step therapy · score 0.70
  > There is no medication step. The gate is whether a home study is a reasonable first test. If the note does not address that question, or does not record a screening score or a qualifying comorbidity, the criteria are indeterminate and the request should be interpreted against this guidance rather than auto-decided.
